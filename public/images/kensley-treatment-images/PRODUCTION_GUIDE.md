# Kensley Treatment Image Production Guide

## Deliverable

- One original image for every row in `manifest.csv`.
- Save each image as:
  - `kensley-<parent-slug>-<sub-treatment-slug>-01-master.png`
  - `kensley-<parent-slug>-<sub-treatment-slug>-01.webp`
- Folder hierarchy:
  - `<parent-order>-<parent-slug>/<sub-treatment-slug>/`

## Universal visual system

Use case: `photorealistic-natural`

Asset type: premium aesthetic-clinic website treatment image.

Scene: a sophisticated contemporary UK medical-aesthetics clinic with warm ivory walls, pale stone surfaces, subtle champagne metal and restrained charcoal accents.

Style: high-end British medical-aesthetics editorial photography; photorealistic; refined but medically credible; realistic unretouched skin texture; natural anatomy and proportions.

Composition: 3:2 landscape; keep the primary patient, target area and any device inside the central 70%; leave crop-safe margins for website cards, desktop sections and mobile crops.

Lighting: soft diffused daylight with controlled clinical highlights.

People: inclusive adult patients approximately 25–65; varied gender, age and skin tone across the collection; clinicians wear tailored neutral clinical uniforms and appropriate gloves/PPE where relevant.

Universal constraints: no text, logo, watermark, before-and-after split, visible brand name, invented product packaging, exaggerated outcome, distorted anatomy, beauty-filter skin, graphic needle penetration, blood, wounds, bruising, burns, dramatic swelling, floating DNA, cells or anatomical cutaways.

## Treatment-family safety direction

- Anti-wrinkle and migraine: consultation or area assessment only; never depict injection or prescription product.
- Dermal fillers: conservative facial-proportion assessment; no active injection; retain natural facial volume and skin texture.
- Skin boosters, Profhilo and polynucleotides: hydration/skin-quality assessment; avoid branded packaging and injection papules.
- PRP and plasma gel: sealed tube preparation or calm consultation; no exposed blood.
- Exosomes and Calecim: topical post-treatment serum application; no injectable exosome or stem-cell imagery.
- Microneedling and RF microneedling: generic sterile handpiece gently contacting intact skin; no visible needles or bleeding.
- HIFU: external ultrasound-style handpiece with coupling gel; do not imply surgical-equivalent lifting.
- Chemical peel: clear solution applied with a fan brush or gauze; no burns or peeling sheets.
- HydrO2: generic hydradermabrasion/oxygen facial handpiece; no copied branded device.
- Mesotherapy: product-neutral consultation or preparation; no close-up needle entry.
- LED: proper opaque eye protection beneath a professional LED panel.
- Skincare: clinician-led skin analysis and unbranded products with labels turned away.

## Quality check

Reject or regenerate any image containing:

- incorrect hands, eyes, device geometry or treatment area;
- readable or garbled text;
- logos or product trademarks;
- graphic clinical content;
- unrealistic skin smoothing or guaranteed-result styling;
- a composition that cannot crop safely to 16:9 or 4:3.
